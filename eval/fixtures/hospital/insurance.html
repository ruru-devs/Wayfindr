TPA Claim Pre-Authorization Procedure

For planned hospital admissions, submit your health insurance card, government photo ID, doctor admission advice note, and policy copy at the TPA desk 48 hours prior to admission. Emergency admissions must be notified within 24 hours of hospitalization.
